How listing requests are handled

Information collected: request type, plugin name, optional ChatGPT listing URL or ID, submitter name or organization, email address, and the details you enter.

Purpose: reviewing correction, addition, description, or removal requests for the independent Plugin Discovery catalog. We may contact you to clarify a request or verify publisher authorization before making ownership-sensitive changes.

Delivery: this form is processed by FormSubmit, a separate provider that sends the submission to padenspinksspam@gmail.com. According to FormSubmit's published documentation, it retains form submissions for 30 days. Messages may also remain in our Gmail account for operational review. We have not set a separate automatic deletion schedule.

Visibility: we do not intend to publish your contact email or private request text as a catalog listing. The public source attribution for a plugin is distinct from this private request form.

Scope: approval of a request affects Plugin Discovery only. This service cannot remove a plugin from the official OpenAI marketplace.

Security: do not submit passwords, government identifiers, account recovery details, or documents containing sensitive personal information. Use the published support email for questions about a past submission.
